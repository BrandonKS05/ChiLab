# ChiLab Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 3, 2026_

ChiLab ("the extension") is a browser extension that checks the correctness of
mathematical writing inside the Overleaf editor. This policy explains what data
the extension handles and how.

## Data the extension processes

To check your math, the extension reads the LaTeX / mathematical text you are
actively editing in your Overleaf document ("document content") and sends the
relevant portions to ChiLab's backend services for analysis. The results
(error highlights, explanations, and suggestions) are returned to the extension
and displayed in the editor.

The extension also stores your own settings and preferences (for example,
whether autocomplete is enabled, and display options) locally on your device
using the browser's storage. These settings never leave your device.

## Data the extension does NOT collect

The extension does not collect or process any of the following:

- Personally identifiable information (name, email, address, ID numbers)
- Health information
- Financial or payment information
- Authentication credentials (passwords, PINs, API keys)
- Location
- Web browsing history

The extension runs only on Overleaf pages (`https://*.overleaf.com/*`) and does
not track your activity on any other website.

## How your data is used

Document content is used solely to provide the extension's core function —
analyzing and verifying your mathematics and returning feedback. We do **not**:

- sell your data,
- share it with third parties for their own purposes,
- use it for advertising, or
- use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

## Third-party processing

To perform the analysis, document content is transmitted to backend services
operated for ChiLab, including a model-inference service hosted on
[Modal](https://modal.com). This data is used only to fulfill your request and
return results; it is not repurposed.

## Data retention

Document content is processed to generate results and is not retained for any
purpose beyond providing the extension's functionality. Your settings remain on
your device until you remove them or uninstall the extension.
